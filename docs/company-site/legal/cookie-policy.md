## 2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts that read information from your browser. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

First-party cookies are set by the Site. Third-party cookies are set by other companies whose services we use on the Site.

## 3. The cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** Needed for the Site to work and to remember your cookie choices. These do not need your consent, and you cannot turn them off through our banner.

**Analytics.** Tell us how many people visit, which pages they read, and where they came from, so we can improve the Site. We set these only with your consent.

**Embedded content.** Our calendar booking tool and video player may set cookies when you use them. We load them only with your consent, or when you choose to open them.

We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies. [Confirm.]

## 5. Your choices

When you first visit the Site from the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before setting any cookie that is not strictly necessary. You can change your choice at any time through the **Cookie settings** link in the footer.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Site from working.

Where the law treats it as a valid opt-out, we honour the Global Privacy Control signal from your browser.
